# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project welcomes contributions from people of all backgrounds and
experience levels. Everyone who participates is expected to help keep the
project a respectful, harassment-free space.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Give feedback about the code or idea, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may decline changes that do not fit the project's
  scope or quality bar, and explain their reasoning.
- Respect privacy: do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Trolling, deliberate derailment, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
commit messages, and discussions, and to public interactions where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or
temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this
policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and explained where
possible.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/ShahabAhmed01). Reports will be handled as
confidentially as possible. Security vulnerabilities should follow
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead.
